What Zefira gives you

  • Multi-protocol sales: VLESS, REALITY, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, WireGuard and OpenVPN — per user, in any mix.
  • Anti-censorship toolkit: REALITY with SNI rotation, obfuscated host, per-user subdomains, CDN SNI spoofing, direct-IP blocking.
  • Business features: volume + expiry plans, start-on-first-use, device limits, templates, audit log, Telegram notifications, backup & restore.
  • Security first: scrypt passwords, HttpOnly SameSite cookies, TOTP 2FA, rate limits, CSRF + CSP headers, session-kill logout.
  • One-click HTTPS: issue and renew Let's Encrypt certificates from Settings or during install.
